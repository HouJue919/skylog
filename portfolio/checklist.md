- Product overview

- Technical plan
- Research and validation notes
- User manual
- Early tester guide

- Working Flutter app
- Local storage
- Flight record form

- Flight detail page

- Screenshots of Home, Logs, Add, Detail, Map, and Profile

- Short demo script
- Early tester feedback form

- 2-3 early private walkthroughs
- Notes from each tester
- List of repeated feedback themes
- Screenshots or quotes, with permission

## Reflection

- Why SkyLog exists
- What was difficult technically
- What changed after feedback
- What the next version should improve
